BLOCKPLAN GUIDE

Tools & shortcuts

Each grid square represents one Minecraft block. Use this guide whenever you need a reminder.

Build tools

Wall lineW

Drag horizontally or vertically for a straight line of black blocks. The live counter includes both end blocks.

Rectangle wallsQ

Drag between opposite corners to place four connected walls. The preview shows the width × depth in blocks.

Single blocksB

Click or drag to paint individual solid blocks anywhere on your floorplan.

Build boundaryO

Draw a dashed outline marking your plot or building limits. This is a guide, not actual blocks.

Doors & furniture

DoorD

Place a door in one square. Click an existing door to turn it, or press R before placing.

WindowX

Place a blue window in a wall or drag to make a row. A window replaces a wall block in that square.

BedF

Place a two-square bed with a marked pillow end. Rotate to position the head of the bed.

Toilet

Place one square.

Sink

Place one square.

Shower

Three squares in a rotatable L shape.

Furnace

Place one square.

Fridge

Place one square.

Bath

Drag between corners to fill a rectangular area.

Table

Drag between corners to fill a rectangular area.

Chair

Place one square.

Counter

Drag a line of counter blocks.

StairsG

Click or drag to mark a staircase. Lines show steps and the arrow indicates which way it faces.

Edit & navigate

Select & moveS

Drag to select blocks, windows, doors, beds, stairs, bathroom or kitchen fixtures and labels. Then drag inside the selection to move everything together.

MeasureM

Choose Line to measure between two squares or Area to measure a room by dragging across its interior. The tool shows width × depth and area in blocks². Measurements never change the saved build.

EraserE

Click or drag to remove items. Right-click and drag to erase with any drawing tool.

Room labelT

Give rooms names like Kitchen or Bathroom. Labels are notes and do not represent Minecraft blocks.

Move canvasV

Drag the view around without changing blocks. You can also hold Space while dragging.

Floors, view & files

Build tabs

Each tab has its own dimensions, floors and blocks. Use New build to start another, the pencil to rename, and the copy icon to duplicate. Click × on a tab to close it, which deletes its saved build. Switching tabs automatically saves your work.

Floors

Blank floor starts a new empty level within the current build. Copy floor duplicates its current level. Show floor below displays a faint guide.

Undo, redo & selection

Ctrl + Z to undo, Ctrl + Shift + Z to redo, Esc to deselect, Delete to delete the selection.

Mouse controls

Left click and drag to place or draw, right click and drag to erase, and hold middle click and drag to pan the canvas. You can also hold Space and drag to pan.

Rotate & zoom

Press R while using a door, window, bed or stairs tool to change placement direction. Scroll or use + and − to zoom. The fit button resets the view.

Temporary ruler

Measurements stay visible until cleared or you switch floors/builds. Use Clear measurement or Esc. They are omitted from JSON and PNG exports.

Saving & exporting

All tabs auto-save in this browser. Save build exports only the active build, Save all backs up every build tab, Import adds saved builds as new tabs, and Export PNG exports the active floor.
